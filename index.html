01 / The laboratory

From mechanism to medicine.

We connect fundamental epigenetic mechanisms with models of development, disease and repair.

Fan Lab investigates how epigenetic information is written, interpreted and remodeled in mammalian cells. Our work spans DNA methylation, chromatin-associated proteins, neural stem-cell differentiation, somatic reprogramming and organoid development.

We are extending this foundation toward RNA modification and delivery technologies, with the long-term goal of developing new strategies for rare diseases and regenerative medicine.

“Understanding epigenetic mechanisms allows us to devise new ways to treat human disease.”

02 / Research programs

Questions we are asking.

Four connected directions guide the lab’s current work.

01

DNA methylation in development & disease

How methylation and its associated machinery regulate gene expression, neural development and neurological disease.

02

Neural stem cells & organoids

Using stem-cell differentiation and organoid systems to model human biology and disease mechanisms.

03

Animal models of human disease

Combining conditional genetics and CRISPR-based approaches to understand epigenetic function in vivo.

04

RNA modification & delivery

Developing mRNA delivery technologies for cells and animal models, with therapeutic applications in mind.
