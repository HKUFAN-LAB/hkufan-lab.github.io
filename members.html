01 / Principal investigator

Lab leadership

Principal Investigator · Professor

Guoping Fan, PhD

Professor Fan leads the Fan Laboratory at the School of Biomedical Sciences, The University of Hong Kong. His research focuses on epigenetic mechanisms in neural stem-cell differentiation, adult brain function, somatic reprogramming and disease modeling.

Research interests: DNA methylation, neural stem cells and organoids, animal models of human disease, and mRNA delivery technology.
